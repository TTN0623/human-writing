### Executive Summary  
During September, we executed the multi-channel "Autumn Transition" paid campaign to introduce our new fall outerwear and knitwear line while capturing transitional back-to-work wardrobe demand. Backed by a $45,000 media budget across Meta, TikTok, and Google Ads, the campaign delivered solid top-line results, exceeding our primary revenue forecast by 14.5%.

### Key Metrics
* **Total Ad Spend:** $45,000 (100% budget pacing)
* **Attributed Revenue:** $189,000 (Target: $165,000)
* **Blended ROAS:** 4.20x (Target: 3.50x)
* **Total Orders:** 2,150 (Average Order Value: $87.90, up 6% MoM)
* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $20.93 (an 8% decrease from August)
* **Blended CTR:** 2.45%

### Key Channel Takeaways
1. **TikTok & Instagram Reels:** Short-form video showcasing realistic outfit layering and fabric drape drove our strongest top-of-funnel traffic. User-generated content (UGC) try-on videos lowered acquisition costs to $16.40, outperforming polished studio assets by 32%.
2. **Google Shopping / Paid Search:** Intent-driven searches for trench coats and textured knits produced our highest efficiency channel at a 5.6x ROAS.
3. **Meta Retargeting:** Dynamic product ads targeting cart-abandoners within a 7-day window drove 27% of all purchases.

### Challenges Identified
* **Inventory Depletion:** Two core knitwear SKUs sold out by mid-month. This triggered ad fatigue on secondary items and softened week 4 conversion rates.
* **Mobile Checkout Friction:** We observed an 11% abandoned-cart rate specifically at the shipping method step on iOS devices.

### Strategic Next Steps for Q4
* Reallocate 35% of creative budgets to contract UGC creators for early holiday gift guides.
* Align closely with merchandising to guarantee 30% deeper inventory buffers for key seasonal items.
* Implement one-click mobile checkout before Black Friday / Cyber Monday traffic ramps up.
